Good to know

Questions

Is the mock-up really free?+

Yes. You don't pay anything and you're not signing up to anything. If you don't want to go ahead, that's fine.

How long does a website take?+

Usually one to two weeks once I have your photos, prices and opening hours. Bigger sites take a little longer, and I'll tell you up front.

How do I pay?+

50% to start and 50% when your site goes live. Invoices are due within 14 days, and quotes are valid for 30 days.

Are there other costs?+

Your own web address (like yourbusiness.co.uk) is usually around £10 to £15 a year, paid to the domain company, and I'll help you set it up. I'll always tell you about any other costs before we start.

Can I update the site myself?+

With the admin panel option, yes: change prices, photos and hours from your phone. Otherwise, just send me what to change, or pick Website Care and I'll keep it fresh every month.
